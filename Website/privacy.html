Privacy Policy

At GymGo, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal and fitness data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, log a workout, or contact support. This includes:

  • Personal info (Name, Email)
  • Fitness data (Weight, Height, Workout logs)
  • Nutrition data (Food logs, Macros)
  • Device information (OS version, device model)

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used strictly to provide and improve the GymGo experience. We use AI to analyze your performance and provide coaching, but this data is never sold to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption to protect your data. Most of your fitness data is stored locally on your device and synced securely with our cloud backup system.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, export, or delete your data at any time through the app settings.
